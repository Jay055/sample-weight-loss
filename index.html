Frequently asked

Before you begin.

Does the assessment prescribe medication?

No. It is a guided screening experience. Any prescription decision must be made by an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Can I browse treatments without completing the assessment?

Yes. The treatment cards can contain approved educational content so visitors can understand their options before continuing.

Can the normal products be purchased directly?

Yes. Non-prescription products can use a standard Shopify product and checkout flow.
