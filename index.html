Frequently asked

Questions before you begin.

Clear answers should be easy to find before anyone starts an assessment or considers treatment.

Does the assessment prescribe medication?+

No. The assessment helps structure the next step. Any prescription decision must be made by an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Can I browse treatment information first?+

Yes. Visitors can learn about each pathway before deciding whether they want to start an assessment or consultation.

What happens to the information I enter?+

In this prototype, the assessment runs entirely in the browser and does not save your answers. A production version would need an appropriate privacy and data-handling design.

Can normal wellness products be purchased directly?+

Yes. Non-prescription products can use a normal Shopify product, cart and checkout flow.
